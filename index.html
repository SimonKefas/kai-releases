Kai

A native macOS app for developing with AI agents — embedded terminals running Claude, Codex and Grok sessions, grouped into project workspaces, with an orchestrator that coordinates them.

Requires macOS 26 or later, on Apple Silicon.

Kai will not install on an older macOS, and there is no Intel build. Check Apple menu → About This Mac before downloading.

Installing

  • Unzip the download and drag Kai.app into /Applications.
  • Open it normally. The app is signed and notarized by Apple, so there is no security warning to click through.
  • Kai checks for updates on its own and installs them in place — you only need this page once.

What you'll need

Kai runs agent CLIs; it doesn't replace them. At least one of claude, codex or grok should be installed and signed in, and Kai will use whichever it finds. Everything runs locally on your own machine under your own accounts.
